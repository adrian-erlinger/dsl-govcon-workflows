# Human Review Checklist

Use this checklist for every draft workflow output.

- [ ] Only approved synthetic inputs were used.
- [ ] Every material fact can be traced to a source file, field, or section.
- [ ] Unknown, conflicting, and missing information remains visible.
- [ ] No qualification, certification, experience, deadline, or requirement was invented.
- [ ] Scores and recommendations include understandable reasoning.
- [ ] Owners and due dates are marked for confirmation where needed.
- [ ] Risks distinguish observed facts from reviewer judgment.
- [ ] Partial evidence matches are not overstated.
- [ ] The draft does not claim complete requirement coverage.
- [ ] The draft does not provide legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice.
- [ ] Questions are routed to an appropriate qualified reviewer.
- [ ] A qualified person has approved, revised, or rejected the draft before live use.
