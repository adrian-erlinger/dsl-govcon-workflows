# Past Performance and Teaming Synthetic Source Packet

> Training data only. Every organization, person, project, role, requirement, partner, date, and fact in this packet is fictional.

Use this packet to practice matching evidence to target requirements without overstating what the evidence proves. The files are intentionally mixed: some evidence is strong, some is partial, some is missing, and some partner signals are exploratory.

## Approved Source Files

Read these files before running the Past Performance and Teaming prompt:

1. `synthetic-opportunity-context.md` - fictional opportunity background, target scope, and capture concerns.
2. `synthetic-target-requirements.csv` - target requirements and expected evidence.
3. `synthetic-evidence-inventory.csv` - available evidence artifacts and their limits.
4. `synthetic-past-performance.csv` - fictional prior-project records, outcomes, and caveats.
5. `synthetic-staff-capabilities.csv` - fictional staff capability and availability records.
6. `synthetic-workstream-capacity.csv` - fictional capacity view for simultaneous workstreams.
7. `synthetic-partner-profiles.csv` - fictional potential-partner capabilities, interest, and limitations.
8. `synthetic-diligence-questions.csv` - open reviewer questions that must be resolved before relying on evidence or teaming assumptions.

## How To Use The Packet

- Treat each record as evidence with limits.
- Cite exact record IDs.
- Keep separate records separate; do not blend partial evidence into a stronger claim.
- Treat exploratory partner interest as uncommitted.
- Treat missing qualifications, outcomes, certifications, or availability as diligence questions.
- Do not determine eligibility, acceptability, qualification, or final partner selection.
