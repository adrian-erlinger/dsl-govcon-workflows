# Synthetic Opportunity Context

> Training data only. This fictional context is not connected to any real opportunity, agency, company, or person.

## Scenario

Synthetic Organization Alpha is evaluating whether its past performance, staff capabilities, and possible teaming relationships could support a larger workflow modernization and staff enablement opportunity.

The fictional opportunity is larger than Alpha's usual work. It includes documentation of internal workflows, hybrid workshops, accessible digital job aids, controlled document review, experienced program leadership, and four simultaneous workstreams.

## Target Scope

The fictional buyer appears to need:

- documentation of at least 20 internal workflows;
- hybrid workshops for at least 100 learners;
- accessible digital job aids;
- controlled document review and approval tracking;
- a program lead with five synthetic years of relevant experience;
- the ability to support four simultaneous workstreams.

## Capture Concerns

- Alpha has a strong workflow-documentation example, but not all evidence is in the same sector or customer type.
- Alpha's largest workshop record is remote-only and below the 100-learner target.
- Alpha has created digital job aids, but the records do not prove accessibility review ownership.
- Alpha's identified program lead has four synthetic years documented, not five.
- Alpha has evidence of controlled review process support, but limited evidence for running four simultaneous workstreams internally.
- Potential partners have relevant capabilities, but none are committed.

## Intended Learning Point

This case should produce a nuanced evidence assessment, not a clean go/no-go answer. A useful draft should identify what Alpha can support internally, where the evidence is partial, and what partner evidence would be needed before a human team could rely on a teaming approach.
