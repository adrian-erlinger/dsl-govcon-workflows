# Participant Instructions: Past Performance and Teaming

## Goal

Match synthetic evidence to synthetic opportunity requirements without overstating what the evidence proves.

## Steps

1. Read the target requirements, past performance, staff capabilities, and partner profiles in `data/`.
2. Note which claims have strong evidence, partial evidence, or no evidence.
3. Review the output structures in `templates/`.
4. Copy the prompt from `prompt.md` into Codex.
5. Ask Codex to create:
   - `draft-evidence-fit-brief.md`
   - `draft-teaming-gap-analysis.md`
6. Inspect the diff and trace every match to a CSV row.
7. Find at least one match that Codex may have overstated.
8. Revise the draft and apply the participant human-review checklist.

## Success Standard

The draft should help a capture team ask better questions. It must not invent outcomes, qualifications, commitments, or customer acceptance.

Both output files are draft internal workflow aids requiring qualified human review. This training does not provide legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice.
