# Expected Output Guide

A useful compliance-matrix draft should:

- create separate rows for distinct requirements;
- cite the exact synthetic section or attachment;
- preserve stated page limits and deadlines;
- identify missing weights, undefined standards, and unclear page treatment;
- distinguish required response content from reviewer analysis;
- state that requirement coverage may be incomplete.

A useful responsibility-tracker draft should:

- connect tasks to requirement IDs;
- use roles from the synthetic team file;
- label owners as proposed;
- identify dependencies and review gates;
- route specialist questions without answering them;
- avoid inventing internal due dates.

## Review Challenges

Ask:

1. Did the draft split multi-part instructions into usable rows?
2. Did it turn an evaluation topic into a requirement not stated in the source?
3. Did it assign security, legal, contracts, or pricing conclusions to Codex?
4. Did it hide ambiguities about graphics or the assumptions-file page limit?
5. Did it imply that extraction was complete?

The files are draft internal workflow aids requiring qualified human review. They do not provide legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice.
