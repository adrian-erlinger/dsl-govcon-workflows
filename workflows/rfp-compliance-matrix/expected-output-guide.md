# Expected Output Guide

A useful compliance-matrix draft should:

- create separate rows for distinct requirements;
- cite the exact synthetic section or attachment;
- account for amendment impacts without losing the original source trail;
- use Q&A answers when supplied and preserve unanswered Q&A as review items;
- preserve stated page limits and deadlines;
- identify missing weights, undefined standards, incomplete workbook instructions, and unclear page or packaging treatment;
- use `compliance_risk` for source-based omissions, ambiguities, mismatches, or uncertainties—not legal or compliance conclusions;
- distinguish required response content from reviewer analysis;
- state that requirement coverage may be incomplete.

A useful responsibility-tracker draft should:

- connect tasks to requirement IDs;
- use roles from the synthetic team file;
- label owners as proposed;
- use the proposed owner for coordination and the consulted role for specialist review or input;
- identify dependencies and review gates;
- use only source dates; use `Needs human planning` when an internal date is not supplied;
- distinguish contracts questions from questions requiring qualified legal judgment;
- omit DevOps tasks unless the supplied RFP contains relevant operational or environment evidence;
- route specialist questions without answering them;
- avoid inventing internal due dates.

The `EXAMPLE-ONLY` template rows demonstrate CSV format and should not be copied automatically into the drafts. General safety language belongs in the workflow instructions and review guidance, not repeated in every row. The drafts should not imply legal interpretation, automated compliance certification, or final proposal review.

## Review Challenges

Ask:

1. Did the draft split multi-part instructions into usable rows?
2. Did it turn an evaluation topic into a requirement not stated in the source?
3. Did it assign security, legal, contracts, or pricing conclusions to Codex?
4. Did it hide ambiguities about graphics or the assumptions-file page limit?
5. Did it miss that the transition schedule changed from 30 to 45 synthetic calendar days?
6. Did it treat unanswered Q&A as though it were answered?
7. Did it imply that extraction was complete or that compliance had been certified?

The files are draft internal workflow aids requiring qualified human review. They do not provide legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice.
