# Opportunity Triage Synthetic Case Packet

> Training data only. Every organization, person, opportunity, date, project, certification, and fact in this packet is fictional.

Use this packet to practice first-pass bid/no-bid triage. The files are intentionally incomplete, just like early opportunity materials. Missing information should become reviewer questions, not assumptions.

## Approved Source Files

Read these files before running the Opportunity Triage prompt:

1. `synthetic-opportunity-notice.md` - fictional early market notice with scope, response instructions, deliverables, and known limitations.
2. `synthetic-opportunity-summary.md` - narrative overview of the fictional notice and bidder context.
3. `synthetic-opportunity-input.csv` - structured fields, known/partial/unknown statuses, and short source notes.
4. `synthetic-review-criteria.md` - incomplete fictional review criteria and reviewer questions.
5. `synthetic-customer-context.md` - fictional customer context, weak relationship evidence, and one conflicting on-site-session note.
6. `synthetic-capability-statement.md` - mock capability statement for Synthetic Organization Alpha.
7. `synthetic-past-performance-snapshots.csv` - fictional project examples and evidence caveats.
8. `synthetic-delivery-capacity.csv` - fictional delivery-capacity data with known, partial, and unknown readiness signals.
9. `synthetic-staffing-and-partner-notes.csv` - fictional staffing and partner-readiness notes.

## How To Use The Packet

- Treat the files as source material, not as a final answer.
- Prefer direct evidence over broad similarity.
- Separate facts from analysis.
- Preserve uncertainty when a file is incomplete.
- Preserve source conflicts instead of forcing a clean answer.
- Route unanswered questions to reviewer roles, not invented people.
