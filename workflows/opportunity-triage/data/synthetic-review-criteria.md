# Synthetic Review Criteria

> Training data only. These fictional review criteria are incomplete by design. They are not evaluation advice and are not tied to any real procurement.

This file represents an early, incomplete set of buyer-facing review signals. It gives participants enough structure to reason about fit while preserving uncertainty about the final evaluation method.

## Stated Review Factors

| Factor | What the fictional customer says it may review | Completeness |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant experience | Similar workflow documentation, facilitation, job-aid, and staff enablement work | Partial |
| Staffing approach | Availability of qualified leads for discovery, workshop facilitation, and review support | Partial |
| Understanding of need | Ability to explain how the organization would reduce handoff confusion and improve repeatable staff practices | Partial |
| Accessibility approach | Ability to review job aids for accessibility before acceptance | Incomplete |
| Schedule confidence | Ability to start by the estimated date and support remote work plus on-site sessions | Partial |
| Price | Not described | Unknown |

## Missing Review Details

- No weights or point values are provided.
- No adjectival rating definitions are provided.
- No instructions explain how customer relationship, past performance, or partner experience would be evaluated.
- No required certifications are identified.
- No accessibility standard, test method, or acceptance process is identified.
- No price evaluation method is provided.

## Triage Implications

- Capability fit can be discussed because the notice names workflow documentation, facilitation, job aids, and training rollout planning.
- Past performance fit should remain partial unless the cited project evidence clearly matches the scope and scale.
- Customer fit should not improve merely because the organization understands the stated need.
- Delivery readiness should account for staff availability, the on-site-session conflict, accessibility uncertainty, and partner commitment status.
- Pricing readiness should remain unresolved because the review criteria do not explain price structure or evaluation.
