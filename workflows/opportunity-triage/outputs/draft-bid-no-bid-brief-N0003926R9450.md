## Opportunity Snapshot

The public opportunity reviewed for this practice run is NAVWAR solicitation `N0003926R9450`, Generative Artificial Intelligence Electronic Performance Support System under CSO `N0003925S001`.

The opportunity seeks commercial solutions for a GenAI-powered Electronic Performance Support System with operations and sustainment support. The public NAVWAR source describes a need to help fleet personnel keep pace with rapid software updates, reduce cognitive workload, reduce information overload, and provide contextual point-of-need guidance.

Responses are due September 17, 2026 at 2:00 PM PDT. Questions were due August 28, 2026 at 2:00 PM PDT. Phase 1 solution briefs are submitted through PIEE, and the public source says emailed submissions will not be accepted.

## Red Flags

- The public opportunity appears centered on a mature commercial GenAI EPSS, while the synthetic organization evidence is centered on workflow documentation, job aids, facilitation, and training support.
- The public source requires TRL 6 or higher; the synthetic files do not document an existing tested prototype or commercial product.
- The public source includes substantial security, access-control, logging, offline, prompt-injection, vulnerability remediation, and air-gapped update expectations; the synthetic files do not show those capabilities.
- The public source requires active SAM registration at submission for offerors and subcontractors according to the NAVWAR Q&A; the synthetic files do not document registration readiness.
- The public source asks for ROM, commercial pricing model, company viability, IP/data-rights position, and licensing information; the synthetic files do not provide enough support for those topics.
- Partner capacity is partial and uncommitted in the synthetic files, and it is not shown to cover the core GenAI EPSS product need.

## Missing or Conflicting Information

- No approved synthetic evidence shows a GenAI EPSS product, RAG platform, software engineering team, cybersecurity/IA team, or sustainment capability.
- No approved synthetic evidence shows TRL 6 testing in a relevant environment.
- No approved synthetic evidence shows clearance readiness, active SAM/PIEE registration, or subcontractor registration status.
- No approved synthetic evidence provides a ROM, pricing model, company viability details, go-to-market strategy, IP/data-rights position, or licensing terms.
- The public opportunity leaves some details for later phases, including additional artifacts, prototype details, acceptance criteria, and some implementation specifics.

## Preliminary Recommendation

**Do not pursue, based on the current synthetic evidence.**

This preliminary recommendation is based on the mismatch between the public opportunity's apparent need for a mature GenAI EPSS software solution and the synthetic organization's documented strengths in workflow documentation, facilitation, job aids, and training rollout support.

The actual bid/no-bid judgment belongs to the responsible team after qualified human review.

## Conditions That Could Change the Recommendation

- Approved evidence shows Synthetic Organization Alpha already has, owns, or is formally teamed with a TRL 6 or higher commercial GenAI EPSS solution.
- A committed partner can provide the software platform, security/IA capability, operations and sustainment support, and required registrations.
- Qualified reviewers confirm that the organization is only considering a narrow subcontractor role, such as content engineering, SME enablement, workflow documentation, or training-support materials, rather than prime delivery of the EPSS.
- Pricing, company viability, IP/data-rights, licensing, SAM/PIEE registration, clearance, and submission-readiness questions are resolved by qualified reviewers.
