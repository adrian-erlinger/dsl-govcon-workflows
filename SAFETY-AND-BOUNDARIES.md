# Safety and Boundaries

## Training Boundary

This training does not provide legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice.

It teaches participants how to use Codex to create draft internal workflow aids, templates, checklists, and prototypes. Qualified personnel must review every output before it is used in any live procurement activity.

## Permitted Data

Use only:

- the synthetic files included in this repository; or
- public information that a facilitator has explicitly approved for a later exercise.

All included organizations, people, opportunities, solicitations, projects, certifications, dates, and performance records are fictional.

## Do Not Add

Do not enter, upload, paste, or commit:

- real company or client data;
- real proposal or procurement data;
- personally identifiable information;
- passwords, credentials, tokens, or secrets;
- controlled, confidential, or proprietary information;
- nonpublic pricing, staffing, technical, security, or legal material;
- copyrighted source material that you are not authorized to use.

## Do Not Rely on Codex To

- identify every solicitation requirement;
- interpret laws, regulations, clauses, or contract obligations;
- approve pricing, security, compliance, or proposal decisions;
- invent qualifications, evidence, certifications, deadlines, or customer facts;
- make final bid/no-bid, teaming, assignment, or submission decisions;
- contact outside parties, submit responses, or operate external systems.

## Required Human Review

A qualified reviewer must:

1. compare the draft with the approved source inputs;
2. confirm every material fact, date, owner, and source reference;
3. investigate missing or conflicting information;
4. correct unsupported scores, matches, risks, and recommendations;
5. determine whether specialist review is required;
6. approve, revise, or reject the draft before live use.
