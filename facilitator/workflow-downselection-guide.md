# Workflow Downselection Guide

Select one, two, or three workflows based on audience needs, pace, and available time.

## Audience Fit

| Audience priority | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Founders, BD leads, early capture decisions | Opportunity Triage | Creates discipline before a pursuit consumes resources |
| Proposal managers, operations leads, coordination | RFP Compliance Matrix | Makes requirements, ownership, and review risks visible |
| Growth teams, teaming leads, capability positioning | Past Performance and Teaming | Tests evidence fit and exposes teaming gaps |
| Mixed audience | Opportunity Triage | Offers the broadest entry point and simplest first build |

## Format Recommendations

### 90 Minutes

Complete one workflow. Opportunity Triage is the default. Substitute another workflow when the audience has a clear proposal-management or teaming priority.

### 3 Hours

The facilitator may cover one, two, or three workflows depending on audience, pace, and time.

- **One workflow:** Use for a deep build, multiple revisions, and organization-specific discussion using only synthetic data.
- **Two workflows:** Use for moderate depth and comparison of two workflow patterns.
- **Three workflows:** Use compact builds with firm time boxes. Completion of all three is optional, not a participant requirement.

## Decision Questions

1. Which recurring decision or coordination problem matters most to this audience?
2. How comfortable is the group with structured files and tables?
3. Is the primary learning goal drafting, review, or adaptation?
4. How much time must remain for questions and governance?
5. Will participants work together or at different speeds?

When uncertain, select fewer workflows and preserve review time.
