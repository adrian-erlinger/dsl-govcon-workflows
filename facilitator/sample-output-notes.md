# Sample Output Notes

These notes help facilitators assess draft quality. They are not answer keys because more than one well-supported structure may be useful.

## Opportunity Triage

A useful draft:

- traces scores to specific inputs;
- distinguishes fit from missing information;
- treats the recommendation as a discussion aid;
- identifies partner, customer, and evidence questions;
- avoids turning unknowns into negative facts.

## RFP Compliance Matrix

A useful draft:

- creates one row per distinct response obligation;
- preserves source-section references;
- separates proposed owners from confirmed owners;
- identifies dependencies and review needs;
- states that it may not identify every requirement.

## Past Performance and Teaming

A useful draft:

- maps evidence to individual target requirements;
- distinguishes strong, partial, and unsupported matches;
- does not invent outcomes or qualifications;
- converts gaps into capture questions or partner-profile needs;
- keeps selection and teaming decisions with qualified people.

All outputs must remain draft internal workflow aids requiring qualified human review.
