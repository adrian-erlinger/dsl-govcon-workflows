# Slide Outline

## 1. Welcome and Outcomes

- Build structured GovCon workflow aids with Codex.
- Practice source-based drafting and human review.
- Leave with reusable Markdown and CSV patterns.

## 2. Why Workflow Assets Matter

- Recurring work benefits from consistent inputs and outputs.
- Reviewable files make assumptions and ownership visible.
- Codex can accelerate drafting but does not own the decision.

## 3. Safety and Boundaries

- Use only provided synthetic data.
- Do not enter real organizational or procurement material.
- No professional advice, automated submissions, or external connections.

## 4. Anatomy of a Structured Prompt

- Purpose
- Approved inputs
- Required output
- Boundaries
- Human review

## 5. The Working Cycle

- Inspect
- Prompt
- Review
- Question
- Revise

## 6. Opportunity Triage

- Pain point and audience
- Inputs and output
- Guided build or brief demonstration

## 7. Diff Review

- Trace facts to sources.
- challenge assumptions and false certainty.
- preserve unknowns.

## 8. RFP Compliance Matrix

- Requirement extraction
- Ownership and routing
- General review gates and proposal-management discipline
- Incompleteness warning

## 9. Past Performance and Teaming

- Evidence-to-requirement matching
- Gaps and partner profiles
- Questions for the capture lead

## 10. Human Review and Governance

- Named owners
- Qualified reviewers
- Approval, correction, or rejection

## 11. Safe Adaptation

- Keep synthetic data during experimentation.
- Preserve traceability and boundaries.
- Obtain approval before broader use.

## 12. Recap

- Structured files beat hidden assumptions.
- First drafts require challenge and revision.
- People remain responsible for decisions.

For a 90-minute session, emphasize slides 1–7 and 10–12. For a 3-hour lab, use the workflow slides selected through the downselection guide.
