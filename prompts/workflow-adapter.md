```text
Adapt the selected training workflow for the audience and purpose described below while continuing to use only synthetic data.

Workflow:
[Name the workflow.]

Audience:
[Name the internal audience.]

Desired change:
[Describe the change.]

Preserve:
- source traceability;
- explicit unknowns and missing information;
- draft internal workflow-aid framing;
- qualified human review;
- the prohibition on legal, procurement, compliance, pricing, cybersecurity, or proposal advice;
- the prohibition on real, confidential, proprietary, client, proposal, or procurement data;
- Markdown-first and CSV-first outputs.

Do not add an application, dependency, live integration, automated submission, or external connection.

First describe the proposed adaptation and its review risks. Then update only the approved workflow files.
```
